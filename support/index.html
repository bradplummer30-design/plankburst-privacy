Support – PLANKBURST

PLANKBURST for iPhone and iPad · Updated 23 November 2026 for version 1.0.3, released on the App Store on 23 November 2026 (if your device still has version 1.0.2, see “Still on version 1.0.2?” below)

Still on version 1.0.2? This page describes version 1.0.3. Until the update reaches your device, ads are optional: the game asks your age band and, for 18 or older, whether to allow ads, and you change those choices in Settings > PRIVACY & ADS; RESTORE PURCHASES is on the Remove Ads card in the Crew Shop. Everything else on this page applies to 1.0.2 too.

PLANKBURST is an endless runner for iPhone and iPad (portrait), intended for players ages 13 and older (13+), with two chapters, THE DOCK and ABOVE THE CITY. It needs iOS 15 or later, plays fully offline, and has no accounts.

Playing

  • HOW TO PLAY in the game's Settings explains the controls and everything you meet on the dock and on the rooftops.
  • Your best distances, coins, unlocked crew members, rocket boards, flying capes and settings are saved on this device only. Deleting the game deletes them; there is no cloud copy, and Restore Purchases does not bring coins or coin unlocks back.
  • After a fall, the Run Over card shows your distance and coins with its buttons: WATCH AD TO CONTINUE (when an ad is ready) or CONTINUE (Remove Ads owners) once per run, RUN AGAIN for a new run and MENU to go back. If the game is closed on Run Over, that screen comes back at the next start.
  • LEADERBOARD (on the menu; new in version 1.0.2) shows one all-time distance ranking in Apple's Game Center. It is called Best Dock Distance: it needs a Game Center sign-in and counts the distance reached on the Dock before a run's first fall.
  • Switching apps, locking the phone or taking a call pauses a run. Tap RESUME RUN to continue; the game never resumes on its own.

Shop and purchases

  • The Original Runner is always free. The twelve crew members unlock with coins you collect while playing (2,500 to 5,000 coins); anyone you already own, bought earlier or unlocked, stays yours. The twelve Wild Crew runners are one-time App Store purchases at the price Apple shows in your currency. Every runner has exactly the same moves.
  • Rocket boards (THE DOCK) and flying capes (ABOVE THE CITY) cost 500 coins each. You can hold three of each and buy three of each in 24 hours; the 24 hours start with your first purchase. One board or cape is used per flight. If the game is interrupted or closed just as a flight is starting, the board or cape is back in your stock the next time you open the game.
  • Remove Ads is a one-time purchase at the price shown in the Crew Shop. It removes ads for good on every device signed in with your Apple Account (use Restore Purchases on a new device). Includes one CONTINUE per eligible run: the same run comes back at a safe spot a little way back, after a short countdown, with your distance and coins kept. Crew members and coins are not included.
  • Purchases go through Apple with your Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Missing a purchase (new device, reinstall, or a purchase that completed later, such as Ask to Buy)? Open the Crew Shop and tap RESTORE PURCHASES on the Remove Ads card while signed in with the Apple Account that bought it. Restoring never charges you again and does not restore local scores, coins or settings.

Ads

PLANKBURST is free and ad-supported, with ads from Unity Ads through Unity LevelPlay; there is no banner. After the first fall of a run, when an ad is ready, the Run Over card offers WATCH AD TO CONTINUE above RUN AGAIN and MENU: watch it to the end and the same run comes back once, with your distance and coins kept; closing it early gives no continue. After every third finished run, tapping RUN AGAIN or MENU can show one short ad first; the game then goes where you tapped (at once if no ad is ready). No such ad follows a run in which you watched an ad to continue, and none appears during a run, while paused, on the menu, or while you buy or restore.

The first time you open version 1.0.3 a short welcome notice says that the game is ad-supported; CONTINUE just closes it. The game does not ask your age. iOS may then ask whether PLANKBURST may track you (Apple's dialog, once). Either answer leads into the game and ads are shown either way: if you choose Ask App Not to Track, iOS withholds your device's advertising identifier and the ads are chosen without it. For every player the game tells the ad network that there is no consent to personalised ads and that sale or sharing is opted out. Your tracking answer does not turn ads off; Remove Ads does (and this version shows no ads in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland). To report an ad you find inappropriate, tap the hand icon in the ad and choose Report ad, or email us with a screenshot. You can change the tracking permission any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland this version shows no ads. Settings > PRIVACY > PRIVACY SETTINGS opens the privacy policy (what the ad networks receive) and the third-party notices.

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase at the price shown in the Crew Shop. It turns ads off for good on every device signed in with your Apple Account and includes one CONTINUE per eligible run. If you chose NO ADS in version 1.0.2: that free switch is gone in 1.0.3, and nothing was charged or unlocked by it.

Troubleshooting

  • No sound: check SFX and MUSIC in Settings, the ring/silent switch and the device volume.
  • Game too demanding or warm: turn on REDUCE EFFECTS and BATTERY SAVER in Settings (Battery Saver draws at 30 fps; the game itself runs the same).
  • A purchase shows as not owned: use RESTORE PURCHASES (above). If it still fails, email us with the item name and the date of purchase.
